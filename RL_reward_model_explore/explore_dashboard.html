Reward-model exploration dashboard

Copy of RL_reward_model/ at RL_reward_model_explore/. Three architectural / training-procedure iterations vs v2_improved_run. Goal: better held-out pairwise accuracy (test fooled and not-fooled), gen-bias closer to the human 37% fool rate, usable score range. Original RL_reward_model/ source was not modified.

Current recommendation

Keep v2 as the production reward model. None of the three explore runs beat v2 on the joint goal (test accuracy + not-fooled + gen-bias) without losing fooled detection. Closest official checkpoint is iteration 1 epoch 1 (a slightly worse v2). Iteration 2’s unofficial epoch 12 had test 60.3% but was not selected by the v2 composite.

If there is an iteration 4: do not add encoder/FP capacity. Change the objective and checkpoint rule: conservatism calibration toward the 37% human fool rate, a not-fooled hinge (two-sided ranking), and balanced checkpoint selection (overall + fooled + not-fooled). Across all three runs the 65% fooled composite is what saves the gen-biased snapshot.

Iteration 1 — Morgan fingerprint fusion

2026-09-07 16:15–16:30 CEST · idea A · dropped (keep-as-component: fusion only)

Why this first. Highest-leverage architecture change already hinted in the original README. Complements GINE with substructure identity, stays a scalar scorer for RL, does not retune FOOLED_LOSS_WEIGHT.

What changed: src/fingerprints.py, GINRewardModelFP (2048→512→256 MLP, concat-fuse), architecture-aware load_reward_model. Same data, player_id split, fooled sampler/margin/weights, time-encoder init.

Criticism. Extra ~1.2M unfrozen FP weights memorized the 3.6k training pairs before fingerprints could act as a regularizer. Epoch 1 is pretrained GINE + a barely trained FP MLP — noisy v2 with slightly more score spread. Not-fooled and gen-bias moved the wrong way.

Iteration 3 — Distill v1 + v2 into original GIN

2026-09-07 16:45–16:58 CEST · idea C · dropped

Why this next. Two encoder/capacity experiments failed the same way. v1 already has test 67.5% / not-fooled 82%; v2 has fooled 82%. Soft-combining their r_pref−r_rej logits changes supervision without adding a memorization MLP.

What changed: distillation loss (--distill-weight 1.0 --distill-alpha 0.5 --distill-temperature 2.0). Teachers are read-only original checkpoints. First launch failed (v1 reward-head index remap + student logit shape); both bugs fixed and training completed.

  --distill-temperature 2.0 \

  --epochs 80

Criticism. Averaging v1 and v2 logits did not transfer v1’s conservatism. The student inherited v2’s gen preference and the checkpoint rule rewarded it (val fooled 72%). Not-fooled collapsed below chance. Wider score range is useless if the sign is systematically wrong on easy real-vs-fake pairs. Soft labels without a calibration prior just smear two incompatible biases.

Decision: drop.

What we learned (for iteration 4)

  • More capacity (large Morgan MLP) overfits Turing pairs in a few epochs.
  • Freezing GINE does not stop memorization: embeddings already uniquely identify molecules.
  • The v2 checkpoint composite (65% fooled + 35% overall) consistently selects the gen-biased snapshot. Iter 2 epoch 12 (test 60.3%) was discarded by this rule.
  • Distilling v1+v2 without a 37% prior made gen-bias worse, not better.
  • Score range did widen (0.09 → 0.14–0.27) in all three runs — that part is easy and not sufficient.
